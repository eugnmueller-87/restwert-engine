# Decision rules

> THE MODEL ADVISES, DETERMINISTIC CODE DECIDES, A NAMED HUMAN OWNS EVERY THRESHOLD.

GENERATED by `restwert.decisions.registry.write_rules_doc` from the rule registry and `config/thresholds.yaml` (version 1). Do not edit by hand; rerun `python -m restwert decide` to refresh.

Every rule is a pure function of plain inputs and the thresholds file. It returns a decision record with the rule id and version, the subject, the outcome, one human sentence explaining it, the threshold that fired (key, value, unit, owner), every input it read, an optional forecast advisory and a hash of the inputs. Records are appended to the immutable `decision_log` and deduplicated on that hash. Nothing in the tool orders, lists, mails or files anything; a named human acts on the queue.

Status `placeholder` means the value is a synthetic design parameter without an external source; `signed` means a named owner has confirmed it.

## R01 repair_or_not (version 1.0)

- Subject: `device`
- Inputs: `serial`, `family`, `repair_quote_eur`, `forecast_rv_after_repair_eur`, `forecast_rv_as_is_eur`
- Logic: Repair a damaged device only when the forecast residual value after repair is at least the family minimum and the repair quote is at most the family share of that value (equality repairs). Value at stake: forecast RV after repair minus quote minus the As Is value of the broken device. Modelling choice: a repaired device is valued at the grade-B marketplace forecast (rv_forecast_current.forecast_rv_grade_b); the As Is value is the family As-Is ratio times purchase price. A device without either forecast is skipped, never decided on a zero.
- Outcomes: `repair`, `no_repair_quote_above_share`, `no_repair_below_min_rv`
- Logged but not queued: none (every outcome is queued)

## R02 channel_choice (version 1.0)

- Subject: `device`
- Inputs: `serial`, `family`, `grade`, `forecast_rv_by_channel`, `fee_pct`, `fee_fixed_eur`, `days_to_cash`, `holding_cost_per_day`, `buyout_eligible`
- Logic: Grade D goes As Is only, other grades never As Is; employee buyout only inside the buyout window after the rental ended; channels slower than the maximum days to cash are dropped, never the last one. Net per channel = forecast RV x (1 - fee share) - fixed fee - holding cost per day x days to cash. The best net wins over the fastest channel only when it beats it by at least the uplift threshold (equality: the slower, better channel wins). The outcome is the chosen channel. The record names the threshold that decided it (as_is_only_grade for a grade-D device, employee_buyout_window_days when the closed window removed the better channel, channel_max_days_to_cash when that filter did, else channel_min_net_uplift_eur) and lists all four with their owners in inputs.thresholds_consulted.
- Outcomes: `employee_buyout`, `marketplace`, `b2b_wholesale`, `as_is`
- Logged but not queued: none (every outcome is queued)
- Carries advisory: yes

## R03 aging_write_down (version 1.0)

- Subject: `device`
- Inputs: `serial`, `days_in_stock`, `book_value_before`, `already_written_down`
- Logic: Sellable stock strictly older than the 180-day bucket is written down to the 180 percentage of its gross book value, older than the 90-day bucket to the 90 percentage. Gross book value = book value before + already written down; the amount booked is the cumulative target minus what was already written down, never negative. The runner books the amount in write_down_ledger once per serial, rule and as_of.
- Outcomes: `none`, `write_down_90`, `write_down_180`
- Logged but not queued: `none`
- Queue priority 1: `write_down_180`
- Carries advisory: no

## R05 price_protection_reminder (version 1.0)

- Subject: `purchase_order`
- Inputs: `po_number`, `supplier`, `has_price_protection`, `price_protection_days`, `price_drop_date`, `price_drop_amount`, `qty_delivered`
- Logic: For every PO under a contract with price protection and a recorded price drop: claim value = drop amount x quantity delivered, deadline = drop date + protection days. Below the minimum claim nothing happens; a passed deadline is logged as expired; a deadline within the reminder lead raises the reminder (equality reminds); otherwise the claim is open. The tool reminds, a human files the claim.

## R06 renewal_alert (version 1.0)

- Subject: `supplier_contract | rental_contract`
- Inputs: `contract_type`, `contract_id`, `counterparty`, `end_date`, `notice_days`, `auto_renewal`, `annual_value_eur`
- Logic: Notice deadline = end date - notice days. An ended contract is expired; a passed deadline with auto-renewal means the contract renews itself; a passed deadline without means it runs out; a deadline within the alert lead raises the alert (equality alerts), escalated when the annual value reaches the high-value threshold. Rental contracts have no notice field: their notice period is the threshold rental_notice_days (owner Head of Customer Success), which the rule reads itself and writes into the record inputs and inputs.thresholds_consulted.
- Outcomes: `expired`, `notice_missed_auto_renews`, `expiring_no_notice_possible`, `renewal_alert_high_value`, `renewal_alert`, `no_action`

The runner lists it in decision_queue as a priority-3 row (rule_id ADV04, subject_type cohort).
