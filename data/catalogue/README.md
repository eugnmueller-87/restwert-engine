# Public catalogue and anchors

Researched 2026-09-13 from public sources by read-only web agents, one per manufacturer group,
then sampled and challenged by three independent verifiers. Every row carries a URL and a date.

* `models.csv`: 233 models (round 4 count, 2026-09-14), the device families a DaaS provider lists (smartphones, tablets, laptops),
  by manufacturer and series, with the German availability date and the successor.
* `variants.csv`: 622 storage or configuration variants, 541 with a launch RRP (gross EUR, Germany).
* `../anchors/used_prices.csv`: 195 used-price observations (refurbished marketplace asks, trade-in bids),
  with condition exactly as printed by the source.
* `../anchors/verification.csv`: 30 sampled claims, verdicts: BELEGT 24, TEILWEISE 5, UNBELEGT 1.
* Slugs not resolved at all: hp-probook-4, microsoft-surface-pro-9-snapdragon-x-plus.

Rules that were applied: numbers never rounded or derived; manufacturer press release before trade press
before price comparison; market Germany, EUR gross; launch date is availability, not announcement, where
both were found; nothing read behind a login or from an API. A cell that could not be sourced is empty.

Accessories are not in the catalogue: no secondary market, they belong in landed cost, not in a residual value curve.
The rental price of any provider is not public and is not in this data.
